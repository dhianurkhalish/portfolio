Muslimah Period Tracker

Muslimah Period Tracker is an Android application developed to help Muslim women manage and track their menstrual cycles while considering Islamic religious requirements. The application combines menstrual tracking features with Islamic-based guidance to assist users in managing their prayers and fasting obligations.

System Preview & Demonstration

Home Screen

Displays the current menstrual cycle status and provides quick access to important features.

Calendar View

Allows users to view and track their menstrual cycle and important dates through the calendar.

Scan Screen

Enables users to scan and analyze menstrual-related information using image processing.

Profile Screen

Allows users to manage their personal information and menstrual tracking preferences.
